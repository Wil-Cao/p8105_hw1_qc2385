## Problem 1

Let’s read the penguins dataset:

``` r
data("penguins", package = "palmerpenguins")
```

The penguins dataset contains measurements and characteristics of
penguins.

It has 344 rows and 8 columns.

The variables are species, island, bill_length_mm, bill_depth_mm,
flipper_length_mm, body_mass_g, sex, year.

The species are Adelie, Gentoo, Chinstrap,

The islands are Torgersen, Biscoe, Dream.

Bill length and flipper length are measured in millimeters.

The mean flipper length is 200.9 mm.

### scatterplot

library(ggplot2)

scatter_plot = ggplot(

  aes(
